### Project Overview

Sales Analysis Project
This project focuses on analyzing a full year of sales data to extract key business insights and answer important business questions.
The dataset consists of 12 months of sales records, which were cleaned, transformed, and combined into a single dataset for analysis.

### Data Cleaning & Prepartion 

Merged 12 monthly datasets into a single file using Microsoft Excel
Removed missing and invalid records
Converted data types (e.g., dates, numeric values) using Power Query
Created new columns such as:
Name Month (from Order Date)
Sales (Quantity × Price)
Extracted City information from the (address column)
Ensured data consistency and accuracy before analysis


### EXploratory Data analysis 
EDA involved exploring the sales data to answer questions,

- Q1) What was the best month for sales? How much was earned that month?

- Q2) What city has the highest sales? 

- Q3) What products are most often sold together? 

- Q4) What product sold the most?

- Q5) Total sales 

- Q6) total Orders

- Q7)Are there any seasonal patterns in the number of orders

  ### Data Analysis

- Q1) What was the best month for sales? How much was earned that month?
  
  analyzed monthly performance to identify the most profitable month. I built charts to visualize total sales per month and compared the results across all months.
The analysis showed that (December) was the highest-performing month, generating a total Sales of ($4.6M)

- Q2) What city has the highest sales?

analyzed sales performance across different cities to identify the top-performing location. I visualized total sales by city and compared all regions.
The analysis showed that ((San Francisco))was the highest-selling city, generating total sales of ($8.3M.)

- Q3) What products are most often sold together?

performed a Market Basket Analysis to identify products that are frequently purchased together. The goal was to understand customer buying patterns and potential cross-selling opportunities.
The analysis revealed that ((Lightning Charging Cable and iPhone)) are the most commonly bought together products,((appearing together in 2,030 transactions))

- Q4) What product sold the most?

analyzed product performance to identify the best-selling product. I compared total revenue generated by each product across the dataset.
The analysis showed that the MacBook Pro Laptop was the top-selling product, generating total Sales  ($8.0M)

- Q5) Total sales

I built key performance indicators (KPIs) to summarize overall business performance. One of the main KPIs was total annual sales.
The analysis showed that the total sales for the year were(( $34.49M,)) providing a clear overview of the company’s overall revenue performance.

- Q6) total Orders

Another key performance indicator (KPI) was the total number of orders processed during the year.
The analysis showed that the business handled a ((total of 185.95K orders)) throughout the year, reflecting overall customer demand and transaction volume


- Q7)Are there any seasonal patterns in the number of orders

The analysis revealed that Q4 (Quarter 4) had the highest number of orders ((62,839))

### Results & Findings

- Total  sales reached $34.49M, with 185.95K orders processed.
  
- December was the most profitable month, generating $4.6M in revenue.
  
- San Francisco was the top-performing city with total sales of $8.3M.
  
- The MacBook Pro Laptop was the best-selling product, generating $8.0M in revenue.
  
- Market Basket Analysis showed that Lightning Charging Cable and iPhone were the most frequently purchased together, appearing in 2,030 transactions.
  
- Quarterly analysis revealed that Q4 had the highest number of orders((62,839)), indicating strong seasona
